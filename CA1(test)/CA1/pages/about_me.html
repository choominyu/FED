    I am very enthusiastic about environmental issues and would do my part to help in whatever way I can to reduce my damage to the environment. Some simple ways that I do so is by using reusable bags instead of single-use plastic bags, switching off the light when not in use and buying energy-efficient appliances when I get the chance. I believe that even a smallest change can accumulate into a big change when it becomes a habit.
