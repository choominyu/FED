    • My Experience

    An experience I want to share with you is changing my family electricity usage habits. My goal was to cut down on our energy consumption by 20% by reminding each other when someone forgets to switch off the light, fan, heater and so on, to turn it off. It might be a challenge to convince them, but I managed to convince them through rational reasoning on why it is beneficial.

    Practical tips That You Can Do

    1. Switch off the lights when not in use.
    2. Turn off appliances when not in use(eg. Water heater, chargers and fans).
    3. Buy energy-efficient appliances.
