Introduction to Energy Conservation

What is Energy Conservation?

Energy Conservation is the act of reducing wasteful energy through cutting down on unnecessary use of electricity and using energy-efficient appliances.

How Much Fossil Fuels VS Renewable Energy Singapore Really Uses

Did you know that that 95% of Singapore's energy is made from natural gas? This is mainly because Singapore does not have the space or resources to make energy, needing other country's resources to make energy. However, the way majority of energy is created is harmful to the environment, burning fossil fuels and natural gases. Thus, in order for Singapore minimise their harm to the environment, the most plausible way is to cut down on our use of electricity.

Successful Stories of Energy Conservation

  1. Swissôtel The Stamford implemented OR A 200-room hotel in Florida had high electric bills. Guests left lights and air conditioning on when they went out. The hotel added an Energy Management System (EMS). This system turns off lights and coolers when rooms are empty. The hotel cut electricity costs by 25%. That means about $100,000 saved each year.

  2. Office Building Cuts Costs with LED Lights An office building in California wanted to lower power bills. They replaced all the lights with LED lights. They also installed motion sensors. These sensors turn off lights when rooms are empty. The upgrade paid for itself in 18 months. The building now saves over $40,000 every year. The new lights also made the space brighter. Workers liked the new setup. It helped improve the work environment. Small changes can lead to big results.
