My Personal Action Page

My Plan

I like to do sustainable things as it gives me a sense of fulfillment that I am doing something to help the environment in my own way. Something that I plan on doing is changing my family electricity usage habits. My goal was to cut down on our energy consumption by 20% by reminding each other when someone forgets to switch off the light, fan, heater and so on, to turn it off. It might be a challenge to convince them, but I managed to convince them through rational reasoning on why it is beneficial.

What You Can Do to Help

  1. Switch off the lights when not in use.
  2. Turn off appliances when not in use(eg. Water heater, chargers and fans).
  3. Buy energy-efficient appliances.

The Benefits of Conserving Energy

  1. Reducing the amount of fossil fuels burnt. This decreases the amount of greenhouse and toxic gases being emitted in the air. Here are the specific gases that you are decreasing being emitted in the air.
    1. Carbon dioxide. It is produced from fossil fuel combustion. It contributes to climate change by trapping more heat in the Earth's atmosphere as a greenhouse gas. To put it into perspective, climate change causes:
      1. Increase of intensity and frequency of extreme weather events such as droughts, hurricanes, flood and storms
      2. Rise of average global temperature, making it more hot and humid
      3. Reduction of fertile soil, making it harder for crops to grow. Thus having food shortages that may be more severe in the future.
    2. Methane. It is a much more lethal greenhouse gas compared to carbon dioxide, being around 25 times more effective in contributing to climate change than carbon dioxide over a 100-year period. It is often released from the extraction and transportation of natural gas and oil.
    3. Sulfur dioxide. Made from combustion of fossil fuels containing sulfur impurities. It creates acid rain by being oxidised into sulfur trioxide and then dissolving in water. Acid rain affects the aquatic ecosystems and agricultural productivity.
  2. Feeling a sense of accomplishment or pride. You are doing your part as a human being to make the earth a better place to live in because you are aware of the effects of your daily usage of energy and are doing something about it. No matter how small it is, you are stil doing something to contribute to reducing the harm to the environment through conserving energy.
