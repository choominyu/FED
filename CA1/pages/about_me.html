About Me

Introduction to Who I am

I am a computing student who is very enthusiastic about environmental issues. I would always try to do my part to help in whatever way I can to reduce the damage to the environment. Some simple ways that I do so is by using reusable bags instead of single-use plastic bags, switching off the light when not in use and buying energy-efficient appliances when I get the chance.

Why I am passionate about Environmental Issues

The reason why is that I am very aware of the environmental issues that we are facing. Here are some examples of what we are facing:

  1. The increasing shortage of chocolate due to the climate change impacts decreasing the yield of cocao. this affects the cocoa industry heavily, causing an increase in prices of chocolate as demand is still high but production of it is decreasing. Thus, affecting chocolate lovers like myself.
  2. The climate clock. It states that we only have around 3 years left until we reach 1.5 degrees celcius to reverse the damages of climate change, if we exceed the threshold, climate change may be irreversible and the effects of climate change will only become much more severe than it already is.

What I Think About Sustainability and Environmental Issues

I feel that sustainability and environmental issues are something that us humans should be focusing on because we rely on the earth to provide the life and materials that we need to sustain our life. If we continue to damage the earth like this by not caring about it, we would eventually not be able to survive on it because we completely depend on it to live. I believe that even a smallest change can accumulate into a big change when it is done regularly.

My Vision for a More Sustainable Future

I envision that Singapore will go green by completely using renewable energy, such as solar energy or import reneweable energy from other countries, instead of fossil fuel.
